## Story shorts (apocalypse / survival / "what if you…")

- **@apocalyptic_shorts — Season 1 of the farm (5:43, 3.1 M views).** "If the zombie apocalypse
  starts while you're on a farm, don't run to town." Named hero (Eli), narrator + 5 character
  voices, rules as cards ("FARM RULE #1"), a countdown ("28:15 LEFT"), diagrams (well pump needs
  power), inventory notebook ("DAY 1 — SUPPLIES"), sound-radius map, distance rings closing in
  (30 M → 20 → 10 → 5), a rescue by strangers, a moral ("Home ain't the dirt, son").
  Look: flat 2D vector, dot-eye characters, sage/cream/barn-red/charcoal, blurred depth layers.
  Captions: heavy condensed white caps, black stroke, emphasis red/yellow, lower-middle.
- **@creepyvids27 (user: "I like this style very much").** "What if you could never die?" /
  "What if your IQ was 10x higher?" Black round heads, big white oval eyes, thin limbs, coloured
  clothes; softly painted backgrounds; persistent title banner + "PART 1" badge; italic heavy caps
  captions with the spoken word in cyan; deadpan narrator jokes ("No, you're still an idiot").
  Motion is limited (held poses, small arm moves) — our version keeps the look and adds real motion.
- **@survivenight1 lighthouse / @trytosurvive84 ice age.** Countdown HUDs (DAY 1 · 6:12 PM,
  "58:28 LEFT", LIFE 1/LIFE 2, DAYS LEFT 7→1), maps with labels, checklists that tick, a
  last-line twist card: "PART 2 · He's not the only one who came back · FOLLOW FOR PART 2".
- **@sfia8229 500 IQ.** Title strip + Part 1, kinetic small words placed near objects, IQ bell curve.

## Explainers / history (the user: "I adore this style… its animations and directing are great")

- **@chess.history — Polgár experiment (1.4 M) / Ivan the Terrible.** Flat vector bobble-head
  characters (oval heads, strong brows, freckles, beards) in 2.5D sets with heavy depth-of-field
  (blurred bookshelves, palace), slow dollies and pull-backs revealing a 3D chessboard, pieces
  popping on, a gold serif place/time card ("HUNGARY · 1960s", "MOSCOW · 1584"), nameplates,
  HUD gadgets (a sun arc tracking to SUNSET, % bars, VS split screen, a REC replay monitor with
  "6 FRAMES = ¼ SECOND", "K.O."), metaphors (daughters as pawns, the king piece falls = the Tsar
  dies). Captions: bold rounded white with black outline, one keyword green/gold/coral/cyan.
  Palette: charcoal #10191A, deep teal #123D3B, warm brown #49301F, cream #F0DFB8, gold #E9B84D,
  coral #F06C59, lime #9BDD4F. Rhythm: punchy fragments ("1994. Judit. 17. Finally faces him.").
  Ends on a question ("So, are geniuses born… or made?").
- **@null.monolith — how to clean dirty money (2.7 M).** Cartoon cat Tom: cash-only pizza stand,
  fake sales, pays tax, the rest is "officially legal". Remake target in chess style.
- **@scroll.engineer — Apple Watch heartbeat (1.6 M).** 3D cutaway of a wrist, green light paths,
  "Magnified illustration" labels, waveform, motion noise vs pulse.
- **@motion.engineer1 — 120 vs 100 km/h.** Dark data-infographic, green vs red cars, quiz
  ("Most people pick this" → "✕ WRONG"), t = d / v curve, braking distance +44 %, "Follow for more".
