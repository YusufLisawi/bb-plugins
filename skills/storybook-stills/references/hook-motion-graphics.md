# The hook: fast flat motion graphics + big centred words (owner rule, 2026-10-31, NOBODY CAME)

The owner rejected a hook that was a 3-D island with a caption parked at the top ("very bad; 3-D is bad; nothing placed above; small motion-graphics components and words; fast"). The rule since then, for EVERY hook (the first 4-7 s of a part):

1. **No 3-D in a hook, and no 3-D island anywhere.** A map is a FLAT 2-D illustration (`island2d`: contours of the height field in flat colours, red pins, a dashed route that draws itself, labels filled in on the spoken word). The real-coastline flight map may stay as a story beat, never as the hook.
2. **Fast kinetic MG, a new small flat component every ~0.5-0.8 s**, each popping / sliding in with snappy easing ON its spoken word: a plane icon with motion lines, a warning triangle, an engine flame-out puff, the plane diving, a splash, a ripple, an island with a pin "NOT ON ANY MAP", a counter 138 → 52, a calendar page (DAY 7), a water drop, two croc eyes, a storm cloud, the satellite messenger, a boat with ONE seat. Show the premise: Part 1 SHOWS the plane flying, an engine out, the dive, the splash on "went down", the island on "hidden island", a radar circle crossed out on "nobody knew where to look".
3. **The words: big, bold, centred a little below the middle (y ≈ 0.77), one phrase at a time (2-6 words), each word popping on its own spoken time, 1-2 key words per phrase on a yellow marker swipe** (WENT DOWN, HIDDEN, NOBODY). Two balanced lines, never an orphan word. NEVER at the top of the frame; nothing parked at the top edge.
4. **A clean dark background** (a soft tint glow); icons only, no painted picture behind the hook. Then cut straight into the first painted picture / code scene.
5. **Trim every pause inside the hook line** (split the hook into `hook`, `hook2`, `hook3` rows so `trim_take.py` tightens the gaps to 0.25 s). Hooks are 4.7-7.5 s.
6. **No text at the top of the frame anywhere in the film.** `stills_build.py` (`lower_text`) moves any tag / speech bubble / stamp / counter / caption a plan puts in the top third into the lower-middle safe area; a tag's pointer line and a bubble's tail still reach up to their target.

Code: `src/kit/stills/hook.tsx` (the `hook` card + the icon set `HOOK_ICONS`), `episodes/nobody-came/hookkit.py` (`hook(slug, ids, starts, hi, items)`: words from the real voice; an item = icon + word anchors `at`, `at2`, `at3`, `outAt`, an optional `path`), `src/kit/stills/island2d.tsx`, `src/episodes/hooklab/HookLab.tsx` (a contact sheet of every icon). Check a hook with a frame strip every 0.25 s (`scripts/peek.sh <slug> <Comp> 0.25,0.5,… 0.25 6`) before showing it.
