STYLE (never change it): the flat stickman look of the references (flat-stickman skill, assets/reference-frames-*.jpg).
- Clean flat 2D vector cartoon, like a polished animated YouTube story. Flat colour fills; at most ONE extra flat darker tone for
  shadows (hard-edged shapes, no soft shading). NO paper grain, NO texture, NO painterly light, NO glow, NO gradients except a flat
  sky band. NOT 3D, NOT anime, NOT photographic, NOT gory.
- PALETTE: muted and desaturated but DARK enough to feel cinematic: slate / teal-grey for snow, metal and night; sand, ochre and a flat
  burnt orange for fields and sunsets; deep slate mountains. One or two saturated accent colours per scene, and they belong to the
  characters' identity pieces (the red beanie, the teal coat). Never pastel, never washed-out light blue.
- CHARACTERS: a big perfectly round head with a pale warm off-white fill (#EEF0E2) and a bold, even black outline; two small SOLID black
  dot eyes set apart; short eyebrow strokes that carry the emotion; a tiny one-line mouth (an open oval when shocked or scared). NO nose,
  NO ears, no hair unless it is an identity cue (a ponytail, a bun, pigtails). The body is THIN single black STICK lines: a stick
  torso, stick arms and legs with clean angular bends at elbows and knees, small simple line hands (a loop or 3 short finger strokes),
  tiny line feet or small flat shoe shapes. Clothing ONLY as compact flat identity pieces over the stick body (a beanie and a scarf; a
  coat over the torso with stick legs below; a uniform). Head ≈ a quarter to a third of the standing height.
- LINES: character outlines are BOLDER than background lines; background lines are thinner and may be dark grey.
- BACKGROUNDS: simple flat shapes, but every place is told by its props: torn metal, debris, scattered fragments, footprints, rocks,
  fence posts, wheat; flat darker shadow shapes under objects; uncluttered around the faces.
- CAMERA: wide shots with small figures in a big landscape; medium shots; EXTREME CLOSE-UPS where the head fills a third of the frame
  (the neck stick and the collar/scarf leave the bottom of the frame: never a floating head); inserts of a line hand holding a prop;
  SPLIT SCREENS of 2–3 panels divided by a thin dark vertical line, drawn as one picture; thought bubbles drawn as a cloud with small
  circles leading to the head.
- NO text, letters or numbers anywhere (screens, signs, maps, papers stay blank or carry a simple pictogram); every word is added in code.
NEGATIVE: no redesign of the cast, no colour or costume changes, no extra arms/legs/hands, no floating hands or heads, no smiling in
fear or danger beats, no pastel palette, no soft airbrushed shading, no pseudo-text, no logos, no watermarks.
