## Editorial architecture

1. Identify the NMS-backed family policy and who distributes it.
2. Explain the seven core consultations, annual limits, telemedicine and dental with precise units.
3. Separate included cover from the paid marketplace and other contracts.
4. Show the app and voice AI as a conditional booking and tracking layer, with clinical decisions left to professionals.
5. Compare prices, networks, exclusions, permanence and competitor products only against current primary documents. The PDF itself asks for this check.

The PDF covers comparative/transactional search intent (articles 01–12 and 25), benefit explainers (13–16), ecosystem services (17–20), booking/AI (21 and 23), family size (22), and buying criteria (24). A headline about a benefit is not proof that the benefit is universally covered. Use `claim-audit.md` for disputed claims.

## How to adapt these drafts

Use the article's question and customer context as a starting point, then rewrite the copy for the destination and current product terms. Do not copy the repeated four-paragraph product block into every page; the PDF itself aims to improve explanation, and the repetition obscures the article-specific answer. Retain useful FAQ answers and internal-link concepts only after checking the destination pages exist. Recheck competitor terms and every uncorroborated numeric claim before publication.
