# The films: case studies

Forty-one films shipped. Read them to learn how the bar is met, and to know what is already taken. Each entry gives the angle, the beat map (seconds at 30 fps), the signature moves, the techniques worth borrowing, and what the film owns. Source code: `examples/<film>/`, copied from the reference project `~/Developer/brainfast-showreel` (full history there). Key-frame sheets: the asset pack's `lookbook/` and each delivered film's `keyframes.png`. Delivered masters: `~/Developer/brainfast/marketing/*/`.

A new film shouldn't repeat anything in the World, Hook or Signature move columns, and its shape and music should differ from the last film made. Borrowing a *technique* is fine: a spring value, a clip-path open, the odometer, the result-card language.

## 6 · The glass box (39.5 s, sleek dark) · `examples/glass-box/`

- **Black box (0–4.9):** a matte CSS-3D cube; its power LED comes on; "?" and "…" drift out.
- **Glass box (4.9–6.9):** the cube squares up; its faces cross-fade to glass; the mark glows inside.
- **Drop (6.9):** a bloom from inside, a sheen across the faces, rings and a burst; the camera **flies through the front face**.
- **Chat Logs, live (7.4–12):** rows stream from six channels; a question glint, an answer, a "New lead" chip.
- **Correction (12.4–16):** Lucas's thread grows out of its row (`clipPath inset`); the wrong answer shakes and flags; 👎; the popover with the real wording; the note typed; the toast.
- **Learning (16.7–19):** Improvements; **Apply Feedbacks**; the correction flies into the glass brain; the knowledge orbit lights (clean ellipse, spokes stop at the cube); "Replaced the previous answer"; a seal ring and lock.
- **Next customer (19.7):** the same question, the right answer, 👍.
- **Scale (21.6–32):** "+1 correction" chips fly into the brain; the web snaps into focus; a Mon→Sun curve; the pull-back into 9 glass agents (Support, Sales, Bookings), with customers streaming in and 6 channels attached.
- **Final hit (32.2)** on dark → "Try it for free".

**Borrow:** `GlassCube` as a material, the dark glass UI, the "open from a row" clip, the clean orbit diagram. **Owned:** black box → glass box, the dive through the cube.

## 16–20 · Five more formats (31–42 s) · `~/Developer/brainfast-films`

- **Chalkboard** (`makes-things-up/chalk.tsx`): an SVG `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` filter gives chalk edges to everything inside `<Chalk>`; `Write` reveals text with a clip-path wipe; `Board` erases a scene with a moving felt eraser. Keep the filter static (fixed seed) so it doesn't flicker.
- **Receipt** (`hand-it-off`): the paper's length is the sum of printed rows (each row feeds out over 6 frames); a camera that starts tight and pulls out as the paper grows keeps the frame full.
- **POV phone-native** (`pov-1147`): captions as white pills, a lock-screen clock that jumps through the night, notifications that flip state in place. Reads as content, not an ad.
- **Webtoon** (`comic`): the whole page is one tall world; `camY` keys move from panel centre to panel centre on the voice. Add a title page so the first frame isn't empty paper.
- **Particles** (`visitors`): ~170 deterministic particles in one SVG; each loops `(offset + f·speed) % 1` and wraps off-screen, so no pops. Flags (`?`, `$`) and a state change after the drop tell the story without extra UI.

## 34–41 · New angles per niche, no code on screen · `~/Developer/brainfast-films`
