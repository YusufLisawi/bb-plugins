# Niche bank — premises that fit the format

Starting points, not scripts. Every premise still goes through the concept gate ([writing.md](writing.md) § 2), and
every fact must be checked against the source named (or a better one) before it goes in the script. "Verify:"
means the fact is the usual official advice. Confirm the exact wording and numbers yourself.

The best premises put a relatable person in a place everyone knows, with one wrong instinct and a real rule that
beats it.

## Disasters and survival (engine 1, sometimes 5)

| Hook (line 1) | The wrong instinct → the real rule | Verify | Ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| "If your car gets caught in a flood, do not try to drive through." | the road looks shallow → about a foot of moving water can float most cars, two feet can carry them away | NWS "Turn Around Don't Drown" | cliffhanger: the water is rising behind her too |
| "If a tornado catches you on the highway, do not hide under a bridge." | the overpass looks safe → wind speeds up under it | NWS tornado safety | dilemma: the car or the ditch? |
| "If an earthquake hits while you're on the 20th floor, do not run for the stairs." | run outside → drop, cover, hold on until the shaking stops | Ready.gov earthquakes | the aftershock, the stairwell door |
| "If you get caught in a rip current, do not swim back to the beach." | fight the current → swim parallel to the shore, then in | NOAA rip currents | a kid on the next towel |
| "If the power goes out in a heatwave, do not open the fridge." | check the food → a closed fridge keeps food safe for about 4 hours, a full freezer about 48 | USDA / FoodSafety.gov | day 3: the neighbour's generator |
| "If you get lost in the woods, do not keep walking." | walk out → stop, stay put, make yourself easy to find | US Forest Service / NPS | night falls, a light in the trees |
| Zombie series: the supermarket, a school, a cruise ship, the subway, a hospital | each place's built-in advantage and its one stupid mistake | real infrastructure facts (water, power, doors, sound, light) | Part 2 |

## Personal safety and crime (engine 1)

| Hook | Rule | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| "If a car follows you home at night, do not pull into your driveway." | drive to a busy, lit place or a police station; call for help | police / government safety pages |
| "If the bank calls and asks for your code, do not read it out." | banks and government agencies never ask for it this way | FTC scam alerts |
| "If your kid gets lost at the mall, do not run off looking for them." | stay put, alert staff, give a description | Code Adam / mall safety guidance |

Keep violence off screen (a cut to black plus a sound) and label suspicion ("police believe…").

## Money (engines 1, 2, 3, 5)

| Hook | Engine | The running question | Verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| "If you win the lottery, do not quit your job tomorrow." | 1 | will the money survive the first year? | lottery commission advice, winners' stories |
| "What if you saved one penny and doubled it every day for 30 days?" | 3 | how big on day 30? ($5.4 million) | arithmetic: 0.01 × 2²⁹ |
| "How $5 a day becomes a million dollars." | 2 / 3 | does compound interest really do that? | a compound-interest calculator with a stated rate |
| "What if all the money in the world disappeared overnight?" | 5 | how does a town trade by day 7? | history of barter and local currencies |
| "What if you only paid the minimum on your credit card?" | 3 | how many years does $1,000 take? | CFPB minimum-payment examples |
